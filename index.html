Common questions

Coverage explained, without the fine print.

What is an excess?

The amount you pay out of pocket before we cover the rest — a €500 excess on a €2,000 repair means you pay €500, we cover €1,500.

Third-party or comprehensive?

Third-party covers damage you cause to others. Comprehensive adds cover for your own car — accident, theft, fire, and weather.

Can I add flood or pool cover?

Yes — both can be added to an existing home policy in the same call, with the new premium shown before you confirm.

Does travel cover trip cancellation?

Covered for illness, injury, bereavement, or disaster. Simply changing your mind isn't included unless you add cancel-for-any-reason.
